About Sathyasiddha Pattanaik

✦ About me

Hi, I'm Sathyasiddha, a product designer & vibe coder. Based in Pune, India.

I'm a product designer with a vibe-coding habit. An idea that starts in Figma in the morning is usually something you can click by the evening, because building it is the quickest way to find out if it works.

For more than three years I've been solving uncool problems with cool people: the screens that make simple things hard, the flows nobody wants to touch. Making those feel simple is the part I enjoy most.

Most days run on more coffee than I'd like to admit and a Spotify playlist that never stops. The music sets the pace, the caffeine keeps it going.
